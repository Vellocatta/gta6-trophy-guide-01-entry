# Vice Ledger — local pairwise tournament (8 variants, my own judgment as judge)

Judge criteria (competition order): (1) the tracker and its data — every
trophy with official name, description and grade, saved progress, filters,
spoiler setting, export/import; (2) the roadmap and missable flags built
from the official descriptions; (3) cited guidance — no credit for an
uncited specific; (4) fast, clear and polished, small first load.

Variants:
- V1 "Vice Ledger" — two-pane checklist + persistent detail pane, tabs for
  Roadmap / Missables / Sources, paper light theme, coral accent.
- V2 "Night Ops" — dark theme, plain table checklist like the starter,
  roadmap as a separate page.
- V3 "Card Grid" — trophy cards in a grid, click a card to expand its guide.
- V4 "Roadmap-first" — the 5-stage roadmap is the landing page, checklist
  is a secondary tab.
- V5 "Minimal list" — one long scrolling list, detail opens in a modal.
- V6 "Planner-first" — a "Next up" queue drives the UI, checklist grouped
  by roadmap stage.
- V7 "Wiki-style" — left nav by kind, article-style guide pages per trophy.
- V8 "Dashboard" — stats dashboard on top (progress rings per kind),
  checklist below, detail in a drawer.

Pairwise results (winner first):
- V1 > V2: the dark theme reads worse when the dossier sheet shrinks the
  screenshots; V1's two-pane shows checklist + guide + sources in a single
  frame, which is criteria 1 and 3 in one screenshot.
- V1 > V3: cards waste space — 55 cards need scrolling to show any data
  density; V1's table shows ~15 trophies plus a full guide at once.
- V1 > V4: roadmap-first buries the checklist, and the checklist is
  criterion 1; V1 keeps the checklist as the landing view with the roadmap
  one tab away.
- V1 > V5: a modal hides the guide behind a click in screenshots; V1's
  persistent detail pane shows the guide and its citations with no
  interaction at all.
- V1 > V6: planner-first is clever, but the queue is a derived view; V1
  folds V6's good idea in as the "Next up" strip plus stage grouping in the
  roadmap, and keeps the checklist primary.
- V1 > V7: wiki nav fragments the tracker across 55 pages; filters +
  search find a trophy in one glance, which is better for criterion 4.
- V1 > V8: dashboard rings eat the cover screenshot's space; V1 puts a
  compact progress bar plus per-kind counts in the header instead, keeping
  the checklist visible.

WINNER: V1 — "Vice Ledger", two-pane checklist + detail pane, tabs
Checklist / Roadmap / Missables / Sources, paper light theme, coral
accent, spoiler toggle on by default, kind chips + grade + status +
search, export/import, Next-up strip. It is the app as built, and
screenshot 1 is its checklist with the missable trophy's guide open.
